# v0.4.1 Remote Sensing Review

Date: 2026-07-09

Review result: scientifically conservative with remaining limitations explicit.

- Official catalog facts remain grounded in Tier A official sources.
- HLS/MODIS intercomparison remains product-level consistency, not in-situ or ground-truth accuracy.
- MODIS scale factor, MODIS QA, HLS Fmask, projection handling, reduceResolution, temporal matching, and aggregation-before-comparison remain validator concerns.
- Mixed pixels, BRDF/view geometry, clouds/QA, phenology, coastal/water effects, and product-definition differences remain limitations.
- Sentinel-1 flood workflows are treated as screening candidates and not authoritative flood-truth products.
